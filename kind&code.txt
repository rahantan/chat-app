Tapi jangan langsung bikin sebanyak itu kalau belum ada kebutuhannya. Lebih baik mulai dari yang memang dipakai.

Cara membayangkannya
Misalnya:

Kind: validation
    ├── INVALID_EMAIL
    ├── REQUIRED_FIELD
    ├── INVALID_FORMAT
    └── VALUE_TOO_SHORT

Kind: authentication
    ├── INVALID_CREDENTIALS
    ├── TOKEN_EXPIRED
    └── TOKEN_INVALID

Kind: authorization
    ├── FORBIDDEN
    └── INSUFFICIENT_PERMISSION

Kind: not_found
    ├── USER_NOT_FOUND
    ├── PRODUCT_NOT_FOUND
    └── ORDER_NOT_FOUND

Kind: conflict
    ├── EMAIL_ALREADY_EXISTS
    ├── USERNAME_TAKEN
    └── STATE_CONFLICT

Kind: business
    ├── OPERATION_NOT_ALLOWED
    ├── INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE
    └── INVALID_STATE
